Earth, Air, Fire, Water: the four elements must be in balance, or the Work fails. In your chart I find Fire raging unchecked and Water nearly dry. You have burned through your energy, your savings, your patience, chasing quick victories without rest or reflection. The salamanders are grateful for the fuel, but the undines, spirits of calm and feeling, have abandoned you. Mars and the Sun crowd together in Aries, hot and headlong. The coming month will drain your purse and your goodwill alike if you do not slow down. Spend less, rest more, and call on the people who soothe you. Only by restoring the waters can you save the fire.

45. The Wheel of the Tenth Arcanum

Outcome: favourable · 112 words

Round and round turns the Wheel of the tenth Arcanum, ringed with the letters T-A-R-O, guarded at its corners by the angel, the eagle, the lion and the bull. Long have you ridden its lower arc, and long have you endured with good humour. You did not curse the wheel; you oiled it. You helped those who were lower still, and shared what little you had. Now Jupiter, the planet that rules the wheel, completes his great circuit, and the rim begins to rise. Luck shifts in your direction, sudden and unmistakable. A chance, an offer, an open door: take it. The wheel turns for those who kept faith with its turning.

46. The Scales of Maat

Outcome: warning · 109 words · Hermetic

In the Hall of Two Truths the heart is weighed against the feather of Maat, and Thoth records the verdict. The scales have been set out for you, and the eleventh Arcanum, Justice, lifts her sword beside them. Libra rules your season, and Saturn, exalted in Libra, holds the beam. Lately you have tipped the scales in your own favour: a deal not quite honest, an advantage not quite earned, a credit taken that belonged to another. Such weights are small, but they accumulate. The balance will right itself, and it will cost you what you gained. Return it first, freely, and you may yet keep your good name.

47. Quicksilver

Outcome: mixed · 121 words · Hermetic

Quicksilver will not stay in the hand. It runs, divides, recombines, reflecting every light that falls on it. The alchemists prized it as the universal solvent, and they feared it, and Hermes, whose metal it is, laughs at all who grasp too hard. Mercury dances through every house of your chart this season, and I see a time of rapid change. Opportunity and confusion will arrive together. You have always been nimble, adapting quickly, and that served you; but you have also been fickle, abandoning good things the moment they grew dull. The coming changes will reward the first habit and punish the second. Move swiftly when the moment calls, but finish what you start, and the silver will become gold.

48. The Veil of the High Priestess

Outcome: favourable · 111 words

Behind the High Priestess hangs a veil embroidered with pomegranates, and behind the veil lies the water of all secrets. She sits between the pillars, a scroll half-hidden in her robe: the hidden law. Tonight she parts the veil for you. Why? Because you kept a secret entrusted to you when telling it would have won you favour. The guardians of hidden knowledge reward discretion above all virtues. The Moon is exalted in your chart, and your intuition is honed like a blade. In the season ahead you will know things before you are told them: whom to trust, which door to choose, when to act. Trust that silent knowing completely.

49. Venus Veiled

Outcome: mixed · 114 words

Every eighteen months the morning star goes into hiding, stepping backward through the zodiac like a dancer leaving the floor. Venus is retrograde, and she retreats through your house of affections. The ancients said that in such seasons old loves return and old debts of the heart come due. You have been careless with kindness: promises of time not kept, affection taken for granted, thanks left unspoken. The cooling of Venus mirrors your own. A friendship or partnership will feel distant in the coming weeks, and you will be tempted to blame the other. Do not. Warm the hearth you let go cold, and Venus will dance forward again, bringing more than she took.

50. The Sun Behind the Sun

Outcome: mixed · 111 words · Hermetic

The Hermetic masters spoke of a hidden sun behind the visible one, a central fire from which all light descends, and the Emerald Tablet names it the father of all perfection. Its rays fall upon you now, but slantwise, as at dawn. The Sun enters your house of hopes, yet Saturn casts a long shadow across it. You worked hard but doubted harder, and every doubt was a cloud across your own sky. The cosmos holds a great success in readiness, but it will only arrive where it is welcomed. Clear away the clouds of self-mistrust, and noon will come quickly. Cling to them, and you will shiver in perpetual morning.
