# CT46.A8.Zoltar — context file

Prototype of a coin-op **Zoltar** fortune teller. Browser, WebGL, three.js (bundled locally). Works from file://.
Harness: Claude harness v4. Built 2026-10-16. **Run: double-click `index.html`** (A8 renames the page from `CT46.Ax.Zoltar.html` to `index.html`), or `Zoltar_kiosk.bat` so the music plays without a first click.

**Lineage:** A8 = **A7** + the user's A8 request:
*"As before, but with the ability to create a video with audio from the beginning through clicking to get a fortune, which holds
for a while then returns. To make a video of Zoltar for others to see without running it."*

## A8: making a movie
* **● Make movie** (small button, bottom right; or key **M**). One press records the whole show and then downloads it:
  1. the intro from the very beginning (title fades in and rises, the cards are dealt into the spiral);
  2. the button *Click to know your future!* appears and beckons for 2.5 s;
  3. it is "clicked" automatically: the music swells, the card is chosen and flies to centre stage, and the fortune window opens;
  4. the fortune **holds for 12 s** (`index.html?hold=20` for 20 s, from 3 to 120);
  5. the fortune closes, the card returns to the spiral, the button fades back in, and 3 s more.
  The file is `CT46.A8.Zoltar_movie_YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS.mp4` (about 35 s), in your Downloads folder. Press the button (or M) again to stop early; what was recorded so far is saved.
* **V** = free recording: start and stop it yourself, and record whatever you do (several fortunes, for example).
* **What is in the movie:** the WebGL picture plus the two HTML parts of the show, the button and the fortune window, painted into the
  frame with their live fades and the window's opening zoom (Metamorphous lettering, justified text, gold drop capital, double gold frame).
  The help line, version label, sound hint, the movie button and the red REC badge are **not** in the movie.
* **Sound:** the music engine's output is tapped (`engine.out` → MediaStreamDestination), so the movie has the music, swell and gong
  exactly as heard. The Make-movie click itself unlocks the browser's audio, so the soundtrack starts with the picture.
* **Speed and resolution (harness v4, no batch step):** it is recorded live as it plays, so the movie runs at real speed. It is the size of the
  page in device pixels: press **F** for full screen first for a 1920×1080 (or larger) movie. 60 fps, about 0.12 bit per pixel per frame
  (≈ 15 Mb/s at 1080p). Chrome / Edge produce **MP4 (H.264 + AAC)**; other browsers WebM (VP9 + Opus).
* Code: `CT46.A8.Zoltar_movie.js` (new). In `index.html` the frame loop calls `window.__zoltarAfterFrame()` right after drawing, and the script tag was added.

## Verified (A8)
Headless Chromium (software WebGL) from file://, `?hold=4`: one press of Make movie ran the whole sequence unattended (intro → button → card →
fortune → hold → return → stop) and produced a video with a VP9 picture track at the page size and an Opus music track (mean −16 dB, peak −2 dB).
Frames checked: the spiral with the card flying, and the fortune window drawn into the movie the same as on screen. Software GL there ran at only
≈ 6 fps, so its test movie was choppy; on your PC's graphics card it records at the screen's frame rate. MP4/H.264 could not be checked in the
build container (its Chromium has no H.264 encoder); Chrome and Edge on Windows have one.

The fortune should be dramatic and use the language of the occult and the esoteric knowledge. Also Hermetic wisdom from time
to time. The fortune should indicate such things as why the signs of the universe compel a certain destiny, and how the actions
of the fortune seeker in the past has ordained success or failure. These fortunes should also be put into a file for easy
inspection. When the user clicks on the button, the animation and audio should proceed as it is currently designed, then when

## A7 behaviour
- Unchanged from A6: always-on music, button position (27.5%), button fade, intro, card spiral, selection flight, music swell + gong.
- When the selected card **comes to a halt** in centre stage, after `fortune.openDelay` (0.6 s, past the arrival flare) a
  **fortune window** fades in (1 s, slight scale-up) over the presented card: dark burgundy velvet panel, double gold frame with
  diamond corner studs, Metamorphous lettering (bundled font), "Fortune XXIII" numeral, title, gold rule, justified text with a
  gold drop capital, and the hint *"Click anywhere to return the card to Zoltar"*. Size: min(38em, 92vw) wide, max 80vh tall,
  font clamp(14px, .62vw + .82vh, 21px); scrolls if ever needed (none of the 50 overflow at 1920×1080, 1280×720, 1024×600 or 700×1000).
- The fortune is drawn from a **shuffle bag**: all 50 are shown once (random order) before any repeats, and a new round never
  starts with the fortune that ended the last one. (In memory only — reloading the page starts a fresh bag.)
- **Closing:** a click / tap anywhere (the window included) or **Space / Enter** closes the fortune (fades out 0.7 s); the card
  flies back into the spiral (0.9 s) and the button fades back in — the inviting state. Uses `click`, not `pointerdown`, so a touch
  scroll of the window doesn't close it.
- **Auto-return:** if nobody closes it, the fortune closes by itself after **`fortune.autoCloseSeconds` = 90 s** (counted from when
  the window opens; 0 = never). This replaces A6's 30 s `select.presentSeconds` (removed), because reading takes time.
- **Guards:** the button ignores clicks while hidden; clicks and keys during the selection flight, during the open delay, and while
  the card returns are ignored; clicks/keys in the first `fortune.minShowSeconds` (0.6 s) after opening are ignored (no accidental
  instant close); held keys (`e.repeat`) never fire; the button's click doesn't propagate to the close handler. **R** (replay) hides
  the window instantly.

## The fortunes
- 50 original fortunes written by Claude (the generative AI) for A7: second person, Zoltar's voice, 102–126 words each
  (average 113). **20 favourable, 15 warning, 15 mixed / conditional**; Hermetic wisdom (Emerald Tablet, Kybalion principles,
  Thoth / Hermes Trismegistus, Poimandres) in 16 of them; the rest draw on the Tarot Arcana, planets and houses, the sephiroth,
  the elements and elementals, and the alchemical stages. Each says why the signs compel a destiny and how the seeker's own past
  deeds ordained it. Arcade-safe: no death or illness predictions.
- **`CT46.A7.Zoltar_fortunes.json`** is the master list: `[{id, title, outcome: "favourable"|"warning"|"mixed", text}]`.
  The page uses it when served over http(s); from disk (file://) fetch is blocked, so the identical **embedded copy** inside
  `CT46.A7.Zoltar.html` (between `/*FORTUNES_BEGIN*/ … /*FORTUNES_END*/`) is used — the same pattern as config.json.
- **`CT46.A7.Zoltar_fortunes.md`** (+ `.md.html`) lists them for easy inspection: table of contents, then each with number,
  title, outcome, word count, Hermetic flag and text.
- To edit a fortune: change the JSON, then run `python3 tools/build_docs.py` — it validates (ids 1..N, outcome, 75–150 words),
  rewrites the fortunes .md, refreshes the embedded copy in the page and re-renders every .md → .md.html shadow.

## Does the audio start automatically? (answer given to the user)
- **Via `Zoltar_kiosk.bat`: yes** — Chrome/Edge are launched with `--autoplay-policy=no-user-gesture-required` in their own
  profile, so the music starts by itself at page load with no click.
- **Double-clicking `CT46.A5.Zoltar.html`: usually no** — Chrome, Edge and Firefox block sound on a fresh page until the
  visitor clicks or presses a key (browser policy; a page cannot override it). The music is already running silently-held; the
  first click or key press anywhere releases it, and a hint at the bottom says so. After that it never stops.
- Chrome may later allow it automatically for a page it has seen played a lot (its "media engagement" score); Firefox can be set
  to allow audio for a site via the address-bar permissions icon.

## Always-on music (A4)
- `music.ensure()` is called at page load: the AudioContext is created and the engine started immediately (3 s fade-in, `CFG.music.fadeIn`).
- Browsers decide whether sound may start without a click. Chrome/Edge normally hold it; then the audio clock waits,
  a small pulsing hint appears bottom-centre (*"This browser holds the music until you click or press a key — or start Zoltar with Zoltar_kiosk.bat"*),
  and the first click / key / touch anywhere (capture-phase listeners) resumes it. A 1 s watchdog and `visibilitychange` resume it if it is ever suspended.
- **`Zoltar_kiosk.bat`** (Windows): finds Chrome (or Edge) and opens the page as an app window with
  `--autoplay-policy=no-user-gesture-required` and its own profile folder (`%LOCALAPPDATA%\ZoltarKiosk`, so the flag applies even
  if the browser is already running) → music from the very first moment. Add `--kiosk` for a full-screen cabinet.
- Test note: Playwright adds `--autoplay-policy=no-user-gesture-required` by default and headless Chromium does not enforce autoplay,
  so the blocked path was verified by emulating a gesture-gated AudioContext (hint shown, key press → running, hint hidden).

## Music engine (audio/zoltar_music.js)
- Key: **D Hungarian minor** (D E F G♯ A B♭ C♯) — Carpathian colour.
- Layers: low detuned-saw **drone** on D with a breathing low-pass (LFO 0.045 Hz) and slow tremor; formant-filtered
  **"ooh" choir** chords (3 detuned saws → band-passes 420/820/2600 Hz, vibrato) changing every 8 s through
  Dm–B♭–Gm–A–(D F G♯ D)–B♭–G♯dim–A; sparse **FM bells** (ratio 3.51) with a 0.62 s echo; band-passed **wind** noise; an
  occasional distant **gong** (inharmonic FM partials + metallic noise).
- Generated dark stereo convolution **reverb** (5.5 s); compressor → hard limiter (−4 dB, 20:1) → output, so swells never clip.
- `swell(at)`: intensity bus ×2.4, drone filter opens 260→1500 Hz, rising band-passed noise sweep 180→5200 Hz,
  tremolo-ed choir cluster (D F G♯ A, high), then at the peak a big gong (41 Hz), two bells and a sub "boom"; settles over ~3–6 s.
  Measured: ambient ≈ −21 dBFS RMS, swell ≈ −14 dBFS, peak 0.76 (no clipping).
- Deterministic random (mulberry32, seed 7). Live mode self-schedules every 250 ms with 1 s look-ahead; offline mode:
  `build(); scheduleUntil(secs); swell(t); startRendering()` (how the preview mp3 and the automated check are made).

## Card selection (rendering)
- Selected card is hidden in the spiral (`c.selected`) and redrawn in a full-screen **hero scene**: PerspectiveCamera
  (fov 30) placed at `(H/2)/tan(15°)` so 1 world unit = 1 CSS pixel at z = 0 (origin centre, +y up).
- Start pose = the spiral card's projected screen position, projected pixel height, and its orientation relative to the
  card camera (`cardCam.quaternion⁻¹ · card.quaternion`) → seamless hand-off out of the scissored bottom-third viewport.
- Flight: quadratic Bézier (lift toward the viewer, z up to 0.35·H), slerp to face-on × 2 spins about Y, size lerp.
- Halo: additive shader quad behind the card (soft ellipse + slowly turning rays), flares on arrival.
- Pick rule: visible card on the table part of the spiral (not freshly dealt, not airborne) with the largest z (nearest viewer).
- Spiral speed uses an accumulated `phase` so it can surge (`spiralBoost` × smooth rise to the peak, then exp decay 1.6 s).
